SENSAI in the Portal
====================

If your AIMMS Cloud Platform account has a **Portal AI Assistant**
feature under its contract (see the :doc:`Usage <newportal-usage>`
page to check), you will see the **SENSAI** icon in the Portal. SENSAI is the AI
assistant built into the Portal. Clicking the icon opens the SENSAI chat
panel on the right-hand side of the screen.

SENSAI is also available in the AIMMS IDE and in WebUI apps. See
:doc:`sensai-chat` for an overview of where SENSAI is available, what
your account needs, and the features of the chat panel.

.. note::
   The SENSAI icon only appears for accounts with the Portal AI
   Assistant feature enabled. If you believe your account should have
   access but don't see it, contact your account administrator or
   AIMMS support.

What SENSAI can help with
-------------------------

The **Portal AI Assistant** feature grants access to SENSAI within the
Portal. The assistant answers your questions about AIMMS and the
Portal, and guides you step by step through what you want to do. Type a question into
the message box at the bottom of the chat panel to get started.

SENSAI can tell you how to do things in the Portal, but cannot do them
for you. For example, if you ask for the log of a session, SENSAI
explains where to find and download it, but does not retrieve the log
itself.

Things to keep in mind
-----------------------

- **AI responses may contain errors** — always verify outputs before
  acting on them, particularly for critical or high-stakes decisions.
- **SENSAI is a support tool, not an expert substitute** — it does
  not replace the advice of qualified professionals or official AIMMS
  documentation.
- **Use of SENSAI is subject to AIMMS's terms and conditions**, shown
  when you first open the chat panel.

Using skills in a conversation
------------------------------

You can ask the assistant to use a specific skill. Type ``/`` in the message
box to see the skills available to you. Each skill shows its name, its
description and where it comes from: **you** for skills you created, or
**platform** for skills provided by AIMMS. Keep typing after the ``/``
to narrow the list, and select a skill to use it in your message.

The list includes your own skills and the skills shared with your
account or environment. See :doc:`ai-skills` to create and manage
skills.

Skills on demand work the same way in the AIMMS IDE and in running
AIMMS app sessions.

Starting a new conversation
---------------------------

Use the icons at the top of the chat panel to start a new conversation
or view your chat history.

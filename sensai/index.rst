SENSAI
======

SENSAI is the AIMMS family of AI assistants. Each one works inside an
optimization application and answers from that application's own model — the data
it holds and the procedures it runs. The numbers in an answer are the numbers the
application itself produces, reached by asking rather than by clicking through a
screen.

SENSAI products
---------------

**SENSAI Apps** adds an assistant to an AIMMS application you build yourself. You
decide what it can see and what it may change, one identifier at a time, and in a
developer session the assistant makes those changes to the model for you. See
:doc:`apps/index`.

**SENSAI in AIMMS SC Navigator** is the assistant built into SC Navigator, for
people who use that application. It is documented in the
`SC Navigator manual <https://scnavigator-manual.aimms.com/sc-navigator/how_to_use/sensai/index.html>`_.

Availability differs by product. Contact support@aimms.com to find out what is
enabled for your account.
